# Agent Trader Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Agent Trader ("the app") is developed by qezawat-a. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

## 1. Data you enter
- Exchange API keys (Bitunix) and AI provider API keys are stored only on your device. They are excluded from device backups and from the app's settings export. We never receive them.
- Your chat messages and market context are sent directly from your device to the AI provider you choose (for example OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google Gemini or OpenRouter) to generate replies and automatic TP/SL levels. That provider's own privacy policy applies.
- Trading requests are sent directly from your device to Bitunix using your keys. Bitunix's privacy policy applies.

We do not run our own servers and do not store your messages, keys, balances or trades.

## 2. Analytics
The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

## 3. Notifications
If you allow notifications, the app shows trade and status alerts generated on your device.

## 4. Data sharing and sale
We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone. Data goes only to the services above, at your direction.

## 5. Security
Network traffic to Bitunix and AI providers uses HTTPS/WSS. Keys never leave your device except to the service they belong to.

## 6. Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all data the app keeps on your device.

## 7. Children
The app is for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy and will change the effective date when we do.
